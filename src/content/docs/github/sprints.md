---
title: Sprints and the board
description: How your team runs two-week sprints with GitHub milestones, issues and a project board.
---

Teams run two-week sprints on GitHub. The full framework is `docs/SPRINTS.md` in your team repository. In short:

- **A sprint is a milestone** named `Sprint N`. Sprint 0 is weeks 1–3, then sprints 1–6 run two weeks each (week 9 is spring break).
- **Work items are issues:** stories, tasks, bugs and spikes, created from the repo's issue templates.
- **The board's lanes are** Backlog → Ready → In Progress → In Review → Done. At most two items In Progress per person.
- **Roles rotate each sprint:** trail boss, review lead, quality lead, demo lead (and docs lead in teams of five).
- **Each sprint starts** with planning on Tuesday and **ends** with a review and retro on the last Thursday. The sprint report (`sprints/sprint-N.md`) is due that night.

In `mule`, the sidebar shows the current sprint, the days left and your issues, and `/standup` and `/retro` draft from the board. The drafts are only as good as your board, so keep it current.
