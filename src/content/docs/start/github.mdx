All work in this course lives on GitHub, in the [`muhlenberg-cs`](https://github.com/muhlenberg-cs) organization. The instructor creates your repositories there for you.

<Steps>

1. **Make a GitHub account** at [github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup) if you don't have one. Use a username you're happy for classmates and employers to see. Your Muhlenberg email is fine as a secondary email.
2. **Optional but useful:** apply for the free [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack) with your Muhlenberg email.
3. **Send your GitHub username** using the form linked on Canvas in week 0. This is how your username is matched to you for grading.
4. **Accept the invite.** GitHub emails you an invitation to join `muhlenberg-cs`. You can also accept it at [github.com/orgs/muhlenberg-cs/invitation](https://github.com/orgs/muhlenberg-cs/invitation) once it's sent.
5. **Find your warm-up repository**, `first-trail-<username>`, under [github.com/muhlenberg-cs](https://github.com/muhlenberg-cs). Only you and the instructor can see it.
6. **Later, in week 2:** once teams are set, your team's repository appears there too, named `cue-2027-<team>`, and everyone on the team can push to it.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Don't see the invite or your repository? It can take a day after you send your username. If it's been longer, tell the instructor rather than creating a repository yourself.
</Aside>
