All work in this course lives on GitHub, in the `muhlenberg-cs` organization. GitHub Classroom creates your repositories for you.

<Steps>

1. **Make a GitHub account** at [github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup) if you don't have one. Use a username you're happy for classmates and employers to see. Your Muhlenberg email is fine as a secondary email.
2. **Optional but useful:** apply for the free [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack) with your Muhlenberg email.
3. **Accept the warm-up assignment.** The invite link is posted on Canvas in week 0. GitHub asks you to pick your name from the class roster, which is how your username is matched to you for grading. Then it creates your own private repository, `first-trail-<username>`.
4. **Later, in week 2:** your team accepts the team project invite. The first person to accept creates the team, and everyone else joins it by name.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
Pick your own name from the roster, and the right team. If you pick wrong, tell the instructor rather than accepting again.
</Aside>
